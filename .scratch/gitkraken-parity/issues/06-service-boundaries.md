## Question

How should self-hosted services, third-party provider accounts and local agent/AI integrations collaborate to supply the reference release's connected workflows?

## Decision inputs

Connected feature audit and runtime findings. Decide service ownership, identity and sharing model, provider credential boundaries, organization policy, provider selection/deployment assumptions and integration acceptance evidence. Existing user choice: self-hosted services first. Features remain in scope even when a service implementation or provider account is required.
